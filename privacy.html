Privacy Policy

Slavic Horror Engine is a personal automation tool. It does not collect, store or share personal data of any third party.

The only data processed are OAuth access tokens for the operator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. These tokens are stored as encrypted repository secrets in GitHub Actions and are used solely to upload videos to those accounts.

No analytics, tracking or advertising is performed. No data is sold or transferred to third parties.
